The Turning Crown

The Six Bells · Three level-10 challengers · Heroic Exhibition

Phone Edition v0.4

Your mission

Win by removing all opponents or claiming the Crown Dais at two consecutive end-of-round checks. For control, at least two conscious allies must stand on the dais and no conscious opponent may stand on it. A contested or missed check resets your streak.

The tournament is nonlethal: at 0 HP, the safety ward removes you from the current bout, alive and stable. Losses do not remove you from the six-Bell season. A Long Rest is available between bouts in this exhibition.

Know the arena

Crown Dais

20 × 20 ft, 5 ft raised. Two allies uncontested at each end-of-round check to build your streak.

Launch Pads · D3 and O3

Bonus Action; DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics; on success land up to 20 ft away. Failure spends the Bonus Action.

Cover Winches · E12 and N12

Action while adjacent; reposition an assigned barrier up to 10 ft along a clear path. Half Cover where applicable.

Prism Console · G12

Action while adjacent; DC 14 Arcana or Thieves’ Tools; delay the next hazard one round or select its lane.

Hazard Lanes · F and M, rows 4–11

Normally fire at initiative 20 on rounds 2 and 4, announced one full round before. DC 14 Dex save, 2d6 Force, half on success. Bell One has an easier schedule.

Ring-out / Safety

Shoved beyond the field? Return near your entry Prone and take 2d6 Force. Reach 0 HP? Removed alive and stable.

All arena devices and safety rules above are original homebrew. Normal 2024 D&D rules apply elsewhere. Referee calls take precedence.
